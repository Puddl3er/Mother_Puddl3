Money Flow Operations

Every dollar's path through Puddl3 — from an employer's bank account to a worker's wallet, and from withheld taxes to the IRS — traced end to end.

money movement revenue roll-up flagged legal risk
01

Employer funds

Bank/debit pull converts 100% to USDC immediately — the whole deposit becomes the yield-bearing Payroll Wallet, not just the eventual net-pay portion.

02

Wallet sits, earns yield

Undisbursed USDC generates yield continuously. Puddl3 takes a flat cut; the rest returns to the employer — marketed as a benefit, not just a cost offset.

03

Clock-out splits the wallet

Up to 2×/day, each independently final. Net pay and withheld tax leave the wallet as two flows that never recombine.

04

Net pay → worker, in seconds

Stellar settlement lands USDC in the worker's wallet almost instantly — the core product moment.

05

Withheld tax → trust pool

Stays as USDC, still earning yield, until shortly before the statutory deposit date. Whether this yield is even permissible is the top open legal question.

06

Remittance

Converted back to USD and remitted to the IRS/state by the embedded payroll partner, acting as Reporting Agent.

07

Worker spends or cashes out

Card spend, free standard ACH, or an instant 2–3% fee withdrawal — the fee and interchange both roll up into Puddl3's revenue.
